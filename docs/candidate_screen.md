# Candidate screen

Scores are qualitative (1 low, 5 high), based on the first-pass evidence—not
probabilities of novelty.

| Direction | Novelty signal | Plausibility | Testability | Prior-art overlap | Decision |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Crossover map with complete mass bookkeeping | 2 | 5 | 5 | 5 | Baseline only |
| Radiator-aware NEP selection | 2 | 5 | 4 | 5 | Baseline only |
| Joint uncertainty selection stability | 3 | 5 | 5 | 3 | Selected as reproducibility study |
| Hybrid NTP/NEP staging | 1 | 3 | 3 | 5 | Not selected |
| Variable-power coupled trajectory optimization | 2 | 4 | 2 | 4 | Backup direction |

The selected question is whether a stated point-design ranking survives joint
uncertainty. Robust optimization itself is established; the potentially useful
contribution is an open, auditable counterexample or replication in this bounded
scenario. The backup is a trajectory-coupled variable-power study, but it requires
validated optimal-control infrastructure before any comparative claim.
