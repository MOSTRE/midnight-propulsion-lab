# Literature and prior-art protocol

Search date: 2026-10-19. This first pass used NTRS, National Academies Press,
publisher landing pages, MIT DSpace, OpenAlex indexing, and Google Patents search
results. It does not constitute an exhaustive novelty or patentability search.

For each search, retain the exact query, source, and screening reason in
`literature_search_log.csv`. Prefer original reports/papers over indexed snippets.
The next pass should use citation chaining from the accepted sources, NTRS export,
Crossref metadata checks, and Espacenet/PATENTSCOPE claim review.

Inclusion: a source directly informs nuclear/electric/thermal propulsion mission
mass, thermal management, trajectory optimization, uncertainty, or architecture
selection. Exclusion: popular coverage without primary technical provenance.
